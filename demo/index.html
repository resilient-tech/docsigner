B-T, B-LT, B-LTA and CCA-LTA need TSA_URL on the server; B-LT, B-LTA, CCA-LTV and CCA-LTA also need TRUST_DIR with your CA chain and reachable OCSP/CRL endpoints.

Used by profiles that timestamp. These three ship their roots in trust/tsa/, so the timestamp validates for the LTV profiles too. Entrust and SSL.com are in the API registry but gate their root downloads, so drop those roots in by hand before using them for LTV.
